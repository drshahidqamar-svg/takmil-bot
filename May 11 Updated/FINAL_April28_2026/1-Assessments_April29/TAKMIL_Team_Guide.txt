TAKMIL ASSESSMENT — SIMPLE GUIDE FOR FIELD TEAM
================================================================
Prepared: April 29, 2026
For: Coordinators, Teachers, Ops Team
================================================================


================================================================
WHAT IS THIS SYSTEM?
================================================================

The TAKMIL Assessment System lets teachers test students on
tablets at school. Here is how the whole thing works:

  COORDINATOR  →  generates a PIN
  TEACHER      →  gets PIN on WhatsApp, runs the test
  STUDENTS     →  answer questions on tablets
  RESULTS      →  automatically saved and visible online


================================================================
STEP 1 — COORDINATOR GENERATES A PIN
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Regional Coordinator or School Coordinator

1. Open this link on any phone or computer:
   https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/pin-generator

2. Log in:
   - Phone: your registered phone number
   - Password: takmil123

3. You will see a form. Fill it in:
   - Select School     (only your schools appear)
   - Select Level      (1 to 11)
   - Select Subject    Math  OR  English  OR  Urdu
   - Enter Cohort Size (how many students in the class)

4. Tap "Generate PIN"

5. A 6-digit number appears. Example:  662590

6. Send this PIN to the teacher on WhatsApp.
   Example message:
   "Your assessment PIN is 662590 — Math Level 1. Valid 24 hours."

DONE. Your job is finished.


================================================================
STEP 2 — TEACHER PREPARES BEFORE GOING TO CLASS
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Teacher (on their own phone)
NEEDS: Internet (mobile data or WiFi)
TIME: 30 seconds

1. Open this link on your phone:
   https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/offline-portal

2. Scroll down to the card that says "Download for Offline Use"

3. Type the PIN you received (example: 662590)

4. Tap "Download for Offline"

5. Wait 2-3 seconds. You will see:
   "Done! 20 questions saved for PIN 662590"

6. Your phone now has all the questions saved.
   You do NOT need internet anymore for the test.

IMPORTANT: Do this BEFORE leaving for school.
           Once downloaded, it works without internet.


================================================================
STEP 3 — TEACHER SETS UP TABLETS IN CLASS
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Teacher (in the classroom)
NEEDS: 5 tablets (no SIM needed)

1. Turn on HOTSPOT on your phone:
   - Android: Settings → Hotspot → Turn On
   - iPhone:  Settings → Personal Hotspot → Turn On

2. On each tablet:
   - Go to WiFi settings
   - Connect to teacher's phone hotspot

3. On each tablet, open the browser and go to:
   https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/offline-portal

4. All 5 tablets are now ready.

NOTE: The tablets connect to the internet THROUGH the teacher's
      phone. The teacher's phone uses its own mobile data.
      The tablets themselves do NOT need a SIM card.


================================================================
STEP 4 — STUDENTS TAKE THE TEST
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Each student (on a tablet)

1. Student picks up a tablet

2. Types the 6-digit PIN (teacher writes it on the board)
   Example: 662590

3. Types their full name

4. Taps "Start Assessment"

5. 5 questions appear one at a time (A, B, C, D choices)

6. Student taps their answer for each question

7. After the last question, the score appears:
   - GREEN = Passed (scored 60% or more)
   - RED   = Needs more practice

8. Teacher gives the tablet to the next student
   (same PIN works for the whole class)

IMPORTANT: If the internet drops during the test, it does NOT
           matter. The questions are already on the tablet.
           The student can finish the test normally.


================================================================
STEP 5 — TEACHER SYNCS THE RESULTS
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Teacher (at end of class or when internet available)
NEEDS: Internet (phone hotspot or any WiFi)

1. On the offline portal, tap "Sync Now"

2. All student results are sent to the server in one go

3. Done. Results are now visible to the coordinator.

NOTE: If you forget to sync at school, you can sync later
      when you have internet. Results are safe on the tablet
      until synced.


================================================================
STEP 6 — COORDINATOR VIEWS RESULTS
================================================================

WHO DOES THIS: Coordinator (any time after sync)

1. Open this link:
   https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/results

2. You will see a list of all assessment sessions

3. Click any session to see each student's score

That's it!

================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================

Q: What if the hotspot drops during a test?
A: No problem. The student can keep answering. Questions are
   already saved on the tablet.

Q: What if a student closes the app by mistake?
A: They need to start again. Their previous answers are lost.
   Keep the tablet screen on during the test.

Q: Can two students use the same tablet at the same time?
A: No. One student at a time per tablet.

Q: Can we use the same PIN the next day?
A: No. Each PIN is valid for 24 hours only.
   Coordinator must generate a new PIN each day.

Q: What if the teacher forgets to download before class?
A: They can still use /assess (not /offline-portal).
   But then the tablets MUST stay connected to hotspot
   the whole time — no offline protection.

Q: What subjects can we test?
A: Math, English, and Urdu. Levels 1 to 11.
   One subject per PIN.

Q: How many questions does each student answer?
A: Currently 5 (testing mode). Will change to 20 in production.

Q: What score is needed to pass?
A: Currently 60% (testing mode). Will change to 80% in production.
