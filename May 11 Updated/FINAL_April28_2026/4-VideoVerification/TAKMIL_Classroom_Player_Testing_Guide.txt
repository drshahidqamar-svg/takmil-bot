TAKMIL CLASSROOM VIDEO PLAYER — TESTING GUIDE FOR FIELD TEAM
================================================================
Prepared: April 29, 2026
================================================================


================================================================
WHAT ARE WE TESTING?
================================================================

The TAKMIL Classroom Player lets the teacher play lesson videos
from a USB drive or laptop folder. The system automatically:

  - Records when the lesson started and ended
  - Tracks exactly how much of the video was watched
  - Captures GPS location of the teacher at start and end
  - Detects if the lesson was too short or skipped
  - Saves results even without internet (syncs later)
  - Alerts Ops team on WhatsApp when lesson starts/ends


================================================================
WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE TESTING
================================================================

  1. A USB drive OR laptop with a video file (mp4, mkv, avi, mov)

  2. Your video must be in this folder structure:

     USB or Desktop
       📁 Math              ← folder named Math / English / Urdu
          📁 Level 1        ← optional subfolder for level
             📄 lesson.mp4  ← your video file here

     OR simply:

     USB or Desktop
       📁 Math
          📄 lesson.mp4     ← video directly in subject folder

  3. The school must be set up in the system with a school code
     (e.g. HajiPirBakshRajhir_A_W24S)

  4. Internet connection for first-time setup
     (after setup it works offline too)


================================================================
STEP 1 — OPEN THE CLASSROOM PLAYER
================================================================

URL:
  https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/classroom

Open this on the classroom laptop or tablet (not phone).
You will see a setup screen on first use.


================================================================
STEP 2 — FIRST TIME SETUP (only done once per device)
================================================================

Fill in three fields:

  School Code:   Enter your school identifier
                 Example: HajiPirBakshRajhir_A_W24S

  School Name:   Enter your school name in full
                 Example: Haji Pir Baksh Rajhir School

  Server URL:    Always enter this exactly:
                 https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app

Tap "Save and Continue"

NOTE: This setup is saved on the device permanently.
      You will not need to do it again next time.


================================================================
STEP 3 — LOAD VIDEOS FROM USB OR LAPTOP
================================================================

  1. Plug in your USB drive  OR  navigate to your video folder

  2. Tap the green button "Load Videos (USB or Laptop)"

  3. A file picker opens — navigate to your Math/English/Urdu
     folder and SELECT the folder (do not open it)

  4. Browser will ask "Upload a file to this site?" — tap UPLOAD

  5. Your videos will appear in the list on the left side

  WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE:
  - Video name
  - Subject (Math / English / Urdu)
  - Level (1-11)
  - Duration

  IF NO VIDEOS APPEAR:
  - Check your folder has the right structure (Math > video.mp4)
  - Make sure the video file is .mp4, .mkv, .avi, or .mov
  - Check the filter dropdowns at top — set both to "All"


================================================================
STEP 4 — SELECT A VIDEO
================================================================

  1. Click/tap any video from the list on the left

  2. The video will load in the main area on the right

  3. You will see the video preview and the "Start Lesson" button

  4. Do NOT tap Start Lesson yet — make sure the projector is
     connected and students are seated first


================================================================
STEP 5 — START THE LESSON
================================================================

  1. Tap "Start Lesson — سبق شروع کریں"

  2. The system will:
     - Record GPS location of the device automatically
     - Start a timer showing how long the lesson has run
     - Show coverage % (how much of the video has been played)
     - Send a WhatsApp alert to the Ops team:
       "▶️ Lesson Started — School Name — Math L1"

  3. The video will start playing automatically

  4. Play the video on the projector for students


================================================================
STEP 6 — DURING THE LESSON
================================================================

  The overlay shows:
  - ⏱ Timer — how long the lesson has been running
  - 📊 Coverage % — how much of the video was actually watched
  - 📍 GPS location status

  DO NOT close the browser tab during the lesson.
  DO NOT refresh the page.

  Coverage turns colors:
  - GREEN  = 80% or more watched (good)
  - ORANGE = 50-79% watched (acceptable)
  - RED    = less than 50% watched (will be flagged)


================================================================
STEP 7 — END THE LESSON
================================================================

  1. When the video finishes (or class ends), tap:
     "End Lesson — سبق ختم کریں"

  2. The system will:
     - Record final GPS location
     - Calculate total coverage %
     - Determine status: Confirmed / Short / Suspicious
     - Send WhatsApp alert to Ops team with full summary
     - Save results to the database

  3. You will see a result screen showing:
     - Video name and subject
     - Total duration watched
     - Coverage percentage
     - Status (Confirmed / Short / Suspicious)
     - Sync status (Synced or Pending)


================================================================
STEP 8 — CHECK RESULTS ON DASHBOARD
================================================================

DETAILED LESSON VIEW (every lesson):
  https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/lessons-admin

  Shows: Teacher, School, Video, Subject, Start Time,
         Duration, Coverage %, Status, GPS Map Link

SCHOOL COMPLIANCE VIEW (which schools ran today):
  https://takmil-bot-production-0f51.up.railway.app/compliance

  Shows: All schools — Ran lesson ✅ vs Did not run ❌


VERIFY IN DATABASE (Admin — DBeaver):
  SELECT
    school_name, video_name, subject, level,
    ROUND(actual_duration/60.0, 1) AS minutes_taught,
    coverage_pct, status, flagged, start_time
  FROM lessons
  ORDER BY created_at DESC
  LIMIT 5;


================================================================
UNDERSTANDING THE STATUS
================================================================

  ✅ Confirmed   Coverage 80% or more       Lesson ran fully
  ⚠️  Short       Coverage 50-79%            Lesson ended early
  🚨 Suspicious  Coverage less than 50%     Too short or skipped

  Flagged lessons are highlighted in the dashboard and the
  Ops team is notified automatically on WhatsApp.


================================================================
OFFLINE TESTING (no internet)
================================================================

  The classroom player works without internet.

  STEP 1 — Set up once with internet (Step 2 above)
  STEP 2 — Load videos from USB (works offline)
  STEP 3 — Run the lesson (works offline)
  STEP 4 — Results saved on device automatically
  STEP 5 — When internet returns, tap "Sync Pending"
            OR the portal syncs automatically every 60 seconds

  HOW TO CHECK PENDING SYNC:
  Bottom of the screen shows: "X lessons pending sync"
  When synced it shows: "All lessons synced ✅"

================================================================
COMMON ERRORS AND FIXES
================================================================

Error: No videos appear after loading folder
Fix:   Make sure folder structure is Math > video.mp4
       Set filter dropdowns to "All" (not Math or Level 1)
       File must be .mp4, .mkv, .avi, .mov format

Error: Subject shows as "General" instead of Math/English/Urdu
Fix:   Rename your folder to exactly "Math", "English", or "Urdu"
       The system reads the folder name to detect subject

Error: Coverage stays at 0%
Fix:   Make sure video is actually playing (not paused)
       Do not seek/skip — watch continuously

Error: Lesson not showing in /lessons-admin
Fix:   Check internet connection. If offline, wait for auto-sync
       Or tap "Sync Pending" button on the result screen

Error: GPS shows unavailable
Fix:   Allow location permission in browser
       On laptop: Windows Settings > Privacy > Location > On
       GPS is optional — lesson still saves without it

Error: WhatsApp notification not received by Ops team
Fix:   Check ops_team table has correct phone number:
       SELECT phone FROM ops_team WHERE is_active = TRUE;
